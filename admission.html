Admissions

Welcome — and get involved

Enrol your child, visit the school, volunteer your time, or stand with our students through a gift. AES is built on community.

Enrolling at AES

Every child is welcome here

AES serves children from kindergarten through secondary school — sighted, low-vision and blind students learning side by side. If you are thinking of enrolling your child, step one is simply to write to us.

  • Kindergarten–secondary, integrated classrooms.
  • Dedicated vision support for blind and low-vision students.
  • Transport options and assistance for families in northern Jordan.
  • A warm welcome for families and children’s questions.

Donate

Every gift — school fees, braille materials, transport for a child — changes a future. Donations fund scholarships and equipment that keep blind and low-vision students learning.

Resources & tools

We share the tools and materials we use — early-childhood aids, sport, literacy and numeracy, and digital skills — with families and caregivers of visually impaired children.

How donations reach children

Contributions support scholarships, braille books and materials, adapted technology, transport for students who would otherwise have no route to school, and the everyday running of the school. Write to us and we will share bank and transfer details securely by email.
